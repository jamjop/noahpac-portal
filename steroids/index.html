Clinical caution required. Equivalency conversions are approximations based on relative glucocorticoid potency and do not account for individual pharmacokinetic variability, hepatic function, or drug interactions. Mineralocorticoid activity differs substantially between agents and may affect fluid or electrolyte status independently of glucocorticoid dosing. Individualize tapering for the underlying condition, response, and adrenal insufficiency risk.

Taper principles from the 2024 Endocrine Society/European Society of Endocrinology guideline; equivalency ratios from standard clinical pharmacology references. Not medical advice.
