Screening tools support—but do not replace—clinical assessment, diagnostic workup, and timely treatment.

2026 SSC: Sepsis and septic shock are medical emergencies. For sepsis-induced hypoperfusion or septic shock, consider at least 30 mL/kg IV crystalloid within 3 hours with frequent reassessment; individualize volume and use adjusted or ideal body weight when BMI is greater than 30 kg/m².

Based on the 2026 Surviving Sepsis Campaign and Sepsis-3 definitions. SIRS/NEWS/NEWS2/MEWS are preferred over qSOFA as a single hospital screening tool; SOFA change ≥2 from baseline indicates organ dysfunction. Not medical advice.
