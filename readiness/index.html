Active-Duty Outpatient Care

Active-Duty Care Support

Focused encounter, readiness, sleep, and functional-limitation workflows for active-duty outpatient care.

No identifiers, storage, or automatic disposition. Selections remain in this browser tab and are not saved or sent to the server. Do not enter names, DoD ID numbers, units, duty positions, or other identifying information. These workflows organize clinical facts; they do not determine PRAP qualification, certification, profiles, deployment status, or fitness for a specific duty.

Documentation support

Functional Limitation Helper

Describe limitations, duration, and reassessment in plain language without making workplace capability, profile, waiver, or readiness determinations.
1

Record the clinical context supporting limitations without including a diagnosis in material intended for nonmedical recipients unless authorized and necessary.

Reassessment and coordination

Scope, policy boundaries, and sources

These public workflows provide structured clinical prompts only. They intentionally contain no local Minot procedures, unit-specific requirements, restricted information, medication whitelist, occupational qualification table, or automatic reliability or profile decision.

  • DHA Health Readiness Support and PHA overview
  • DHA Periodic Health Assessment components
  • 2025 VA/DoD Insomnia and OSA clinical practice guideline resources
  • AFI 48-133, Duty Limiting Conditions
  • DoDM 5210.42 with DAFMAN 13-501 supplement

Use current DAF, DoD, DHA, VA/DoD CPG, and local guidance. The appropriate clinical, command, CMA, profiling, and certifying authorities retain their respective responsibilities.
